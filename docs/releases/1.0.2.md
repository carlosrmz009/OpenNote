## What is new

**Make a video of any piece.** Press **Make video…** on the bar at the bottom. Choose the whole piece
or just the section you are practising, a quality, and a speed. Each video starts with a two-second
"Made with OpenNote" card (you can turn it off). If your computer has no ffmpeg, OpenNote offers to
download it on Windows and Linux.

**A real piano sound.** OpenNote now plays the SoundFont [JH]Studio Yamaha C7 by 陈俊豪 by default, in the viewer
and in your videos. It is licensed under the Free Art License 1.3.

**More kinds of score.** Besides MusicXML and MIDI, OpenNote opens ABC (`.abc`), Humdrum (`.krn`),
MEI (`.mei`) and Plaine & Easie (`.pae`) files.

**A friendlier first run.** A short welcome shows how it works, and a new start screen asks for a
piece, a colour for each hand, and how expressive the playing should be.

**A new look.** A calmer, cleaner interface with a big round play button, and a progress bar where you
can drag the A and B flags to practise one passage. The hands are blue (left) and yellow (right) by
default.

**Sharper and steadier.** The keys and hands no longer blur or shimmer while they move.

**On Linux,** the AppImage offers once to add OpenNote to your applications menu.

**Updates inside the app.** When a newer OpenNote is out, a yellow **Update!** button appears when
you open it.
